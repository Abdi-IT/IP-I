CSS Background Properties

Resize your browser window to see how percentages behave differently from fixed lengths.

1. background-color: steelblue

Just a solid color – no image.

2. background-image + position: 50% 50% (percentages)

The center of the image aligns with the center of the box.

Resize the box (or window) – the image stays centered proportionally.

3. background-image + position: 20px 30px (lengths)

The top‑left corner of the image is exactly 20px from left, 30px from top.

Resize the box – the offset does NOT change (fixed px).

4. background-repeat: repeat-x

Image repeats only horizontally.

5. background-position: right bottom

Same as 100% 100% – image sits at bottom‑right corner.

6. background-attachment: fixed

Scroll the page – this background stays still relative to the browser window.

Keep scrolling to see it in action.

Percentages: 25% 25%

Image point at 25% from its left/top aligns with box point at 25% from its left/top.

Changes with box size.

Lengths: 50px 50px

Image top‑left corner placed at 50px from left, 50px from top.

Fixed offset, does not scale.

Scroll back up to see the "fixed" background stay still.
